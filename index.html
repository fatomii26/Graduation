How to Purchase Your Tickets

Tickets are non-refundable once purchased. You can pay using either method below.

Online, on This Page

Check your eligibility above, choose your tickets and your guests' seats, then pay through the secure payment portal. The payment is confirmed to this website straight away, and your e-tickets are issued and emailed to you at once.

Finance Office, Dubai Knowledge Park

Pay by card over the counter, or in cash via the cash deposit machine (CDM), at the Finance Office, Block 16. Please deposit the exact amount. The CDM will not process a balance amount. Have the graduate's full name, MISIS number and email ready. You will receive a receipt number. Enter it with your MISIS on this page, under "Paid at the Finance Office?", to choose your guests' seats and receive your e-tickets.

Your E-Tickets

There are no paper tickets. Every person attending has their own e-ticket with a QR code: one for the graduate and one for each guest. Your e-tickets appear on screen as soon as your payment is confirmed. From there you can download them all as a PDF, save each one as an image, or email them to yourself or your guests. You can view them again at any time by checking your eligibility above and choosing "View My E-Tickets". If a ticket is lost, the Marketing team can send it again.

Please send each guest their own e-ticket. Guest QR codes are scanned twice: once at the venue entrance and once at the hall door, so do not share or copy them. Graduate QR codes can be scanned as often as needed. Guest e-tickets show the reserved seat. Graduates sit in front, grouped by programme. Their seats are allocated the day before the ceremony in the order graduates paid, and the seat code, for example GRAD-LAW-B2, shows on the e-ticket and when it is scanned.

Choosing Guest Seats

Guest seats are chosen on a seating plan of the hall when you buy your tickets. Seats are first come, first served, so the front rows fill first. Once you choose your seats, they are held for 10 minutes while you pay. If payment is not completed in time, the seats are released for others.

Guest seats cannot be changed after payment. The only exception is a transfer of guest tickets to another graduate in the same ceremony. You can request this from "My E-Tickets", and Marketing reviews each request. The seat moves with the ticket.

Collect Your Graduation Package

Collect your graduation package outside the Marketing Office, 3rd Floor, Block 16, Dubai Knowledge Park.

Monday – Thursday: 9:00 AM – 6:30 PM  ·  Friday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Your package includes your cap, gown and scarf for your ceremony, and a laminated name card (bring this with you on the day). International graduates travelling for the event may collect their package on arrival, a few days before the ceremony.

Venue & Parking

Traffic on the Palm is busy at weekends. Please allow plenty of time, as latecomers cannot be admitted once doors close. Arriving by public transport is encouraged.

Free valet parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis at Atlantis The Royal for the duration of your ceremony. Go to the Conference Centre entrance to request it.

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Guest tickets can only be purchased after the graduate ticket has been bought.
  • Maximum 1 graduate ticket and 2 guest tickets per student, at this stage.
  • Do not attempt to purchase more than 2 guest tickets. Any surplus balance will be refunded and no extra ticket issued.
  • Children under 4 may attend free of charge. They do not need an e-ticket, are not allocated a seat, and must sit with a parent or guardian.
  • Guest QR codes work once at the venue entrance and once at the hall door. Graduate QR codes work at every scan. Keep them private.
  • Guest seats cannot be changed after payment, except by an approved transfer to another graduate in the same ceremony.
  • Students on financial hold cannot purchase tickets until their balance is cleared, unless approved by Finance or Marketing.
  • For eligibility queries, contact Campus Central via AskMDX. For graduation logistics, contact graduation@mdx.ac.ae.
Important to note: Communications about Graduation Class of 2026 are for planning purposes only and are not formal confirmation of award. Only students formally awarded their qualification, having met all academic and regulatory requirements, may attend. Formal confirmation of award is issued separately via MyMDX. Attending a ceremony, appearing in ceremony materials, purchasing gowns, or event photography does not constitute evidence of graduation.
