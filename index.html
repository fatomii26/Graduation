How to Purchase Your Tickets

Tickets are non-refundable once purchased. You can pay using either method below.

Finance Office, Dubai Knowledge Park

Pay in cash via the cash deposit machine (CDM) or by card over the counter at the Finance Office, Block 16. Please deposit the exact amount. The CDM will not process a balance amount. Have the graduate's full name, MISIS number and email ready.

MDX Online Payment Portal

Pay online and reference the graduate's full name, MISIS number and email. Select "Graduation" under 'Are you', then choose your ticket option. Online payments take 48 hours (2 working days) to reflect on our records before tickets can be issued.

How to Collect Your Tickets

Collect your tickets and graduation package outside the Marketing Office, 3rd Floor, Block 16, Dubai Knowledge Park.

Monday – Thursday: 9:00 AM – 6:30 PM  ·  Friday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Your package includes: graduate and guest tickets purchased, cap/gown/scarf for your ceremony, and a laminated name card (bring this with you on the day). International graduates travelling for the event may collect their package on arrival, a few days before the ceremony.

Venue & Parking

Traffic on the Palm is busy at weekends. Please allow plenty of time, as latecomers cannot be admitted once doors close. Arriving by public transport is encouraged.

Free valet parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis at Atlantis The Royal for the duration of your ceremony. Go to the Conference Centre entrance to request it.

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Guest tickets can only be purchased after the graduate ticket has been bought.
  • Maximum 1 graduate ticket and 2 guest tickets per student, at this stage.
  • Do not attempt to purchase more than 2 guest tickets. Any surplus balance will be refunded and no extra ticket issued.
  • Children under 4 may attend free of charge but will not be allocated a seat, and must sit with a parent or guardian.
  • Students on financial hold cannot purchase tickets until their balance is cleared, unless approved by Finance or Marketing.
  • For eligibility queries, contact Campus Central via AskMDX. For graduation logistics, contact graduation@mdx.ac.ae.
Important to note: Communications about Graduation Class of 2026 are for planning purposes only and are not formal confirmation of award. Only students formally awarded their qualification, having met all academic and regulatory requirements, may attend. Formal confirmation of award is issued separately via MyMDX. Attending a ceremony, appearing in ceremony materials, purchasing gowns, or event photography does not constitute evidence of graduation.
